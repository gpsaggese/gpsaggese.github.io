Part 2: Bias-Variance Decomposition¶

Cell 2.1: Learning: bias-variance decomposition¶

Goal:

  • Repeat Cell 1.2’s experiment across many random training sets, and read bias and variance off how the resulting models scatter around fff

Implementation: cell3_learning_bias_variance()

  • Runs N_experiments independent draws of N_samples points, refitting g0g_0g0​ and g1g_1g1​ each time
  • Overlays every fitted model in translucent lines, plus the average model as a dashed line, so bias (average vs fff) and variance (spread around the average) are both visible at once

Usage

  • Inputs

    • N_samples: training points per experiment, 2-20

  • Panels

    • Constant models: f(x)f(x)f(x), every fitted g0g_0g0​, and their average
    • Linear models: f(x)f(x)f(x), every fitted g1g_1g1​, and their average
    • Comments: average EinE_{in}Ein​/EoutE_{out}Eout​ and the bias-variance reading for both models
