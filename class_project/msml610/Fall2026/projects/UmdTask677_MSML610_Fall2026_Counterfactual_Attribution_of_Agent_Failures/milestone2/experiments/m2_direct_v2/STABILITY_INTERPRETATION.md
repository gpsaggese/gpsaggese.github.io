# Stability interpretation

The two full-profile repeats use 30 fresh continuations per arm at each decision. They were selected before new primary outcomes.

- krk-m3-1 trial 0: rank agreement 0.986; mean absolute gain difference 0.038; maximum absolute step-level difference 0.200.
- krk-m6-1 trial 0: rank agreement 0.798; mean absolute gain difference 0.067; maximum absolute step-level difference 0.200.

Many late decision gains are zero in both samples. Those flat tails contribute strongly to rank agreement and low average differences. The displayed values do not establish that individual early-step effect magnitudes are equal. Step-level conservative intervals are in stability_summary.json.

The large krk-m4-1 effects were not included in the predeclared repeat tasks. They remain primary discoveries, not independently replicated discoveries. No repeat-based extra sampling or stopping was used.
