  Dear Madam / Sir,

  I am applying for positions at MBZUAI focused on teaching AI and supporting
  the university's entrepreneurial efforts. I am a technologist, founder, and
  educator with more than 20 years of experience building production AI systems
  and companies. I currently lead machine learning research on causal AI at
  \href{https://www.adia.ae/}{ADIA} in Abu Dhabi, and I want to deepen my
  contribution to the UAE's AI community at a university devoted entirely to AI.

  As an \textbf{adjunct professor}, since 2023 I have been teaching
  \href{https://docs.google.com/document/d/1LYd7QqGOU2_JR5QOWaAxWCwBB2cy16n57dSaVV6A3tc/edit?tab=t.0\#heading=h.d1dedvxmz9pw}{DATA605}
  (Big Data Systems, about 300 students per year) and
  \href{https://docs.google.com/document/d/1YXCrqh6KGg3xm4-Lr4QGdBnjeWEfkqz67FHNHB_rdAk/edit?tab=t.0\#heading=h.278ryn4xodsb}{MSML610}
  (Advanced Machine Learning, about 120 students per year), at the
  \href{https://cmns.umd.edu/people/giacinto-paolo-saggese}{University of
  Maryland, College Park} (USA), where I teach topics ranging from distributed
  data systems to Bayesian and causal machine learning. Student feedback on the
  classes has been 97\% positive over five years. A PhD in Electrical and
  Computer Engineering from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and
  \href{https://gpsaggese.github.io/05_publications/}{more than 30 publications}
  give me the research foundation to teach these topics at the graduate level.

  As an \textbf{entrepreneur}: I have built 3 companies from first prototype to
  exit, raising almost \$10M and selling them for a combined \$30M. ZeroSoft
  built simulation technology that made IC verification 3--10x faster, won
  customers including NVIDIA and Cisco, produced two patents, and was acquired
  by Synopsys. I then co-founded June Inc. (Series A/B, \$4M), which was
  acquired by a hedge fund. Today I am co-founder and CTO of Causify.ai, where I
  supported a \$5M fundraise and the product strategy to \$1M ARR.

  As a \textbf{startup advisor}, I have been an instructor for
  \href{https://www.nsf.gov/funding/initiatives/i-corps}{NSF I-Corps}, the
  premier program of the National Science Foundation in the USA to commercialize
  academic research. I advised more than 60 startups in evaluating markets and
  finding a commercialization path for their work. Together they raised more
  than \$10M in pre-seed and seed financing. Besides AI, I am also active in
  digital currency ecosystems and I am a mentor for the
  \href{https://www.rhsmith.umd.edu/centers-initiatives/supply-chain-management-center/initiatives-programs/blockchain-accelerator}{UMD
  Blockchain Accelerator}.

  As a \textbf{researcher and engineer} I have worked across hardware,
  software, and finance. After a postdoc at the University of Illinois
  Urbana-Champaign, I was Senior Software Architect at NVIDIA and Senior Member
  of Technical Staff at Synopsys. I then moved to quantitative finance and
  machine learning as Head of Alt-Data Group at Teza Technologies and Portfolio
  Manager at Engineers Gate. Today I am Machine Learning Research Lead at ADIA
  Lab.

  I would bring to MBZUAI a combination of classroom experience at scale, a
  research record in causal AI and machine learning systems, and first-hand
  experience of founding, funding, and growing deep-technology companies. I am
  deeply invested in the UAE's growth. Since I started working in Abu Dhabi, I
  have seen the energy and potential of its AI community up close, and I want to
  help shape its next stage through teaching and research, using my experience
  accumulated in the USA. I would welcome the chance to discuss how I can
  contribute to your teaching mission and to the ventures of your students and
  researchers.

  My \href{https://gpsaggese.github.io/02_cv/}{CV} is attached. Thank you for
  your time and consideration.

  Sincerely,

  Giacinto Paolo (GP) Saggese, PhD
