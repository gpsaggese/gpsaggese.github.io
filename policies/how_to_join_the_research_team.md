# How to Join GP's AI Research Group

## Take a Class

- Take one of GP's classes, DATA605 or MSML610, and do a good job

## Learn About Our Research

- Review the kind of research we do:
  - [GP's AI research](https://github.com/gpsaggese/gpsaggese.github.io)
  - [Papers](https://github.com/gpsaggese/gpsaggese.github.io/blob/master/website/docs/06_research.md)
  - [Blog](https://gpsaggese.github.io/blog/)
  - [Past projects](https://github.com/gpsaggese/gpsaggese.github.io/blob/master/class_project/project_descriptions/README.md)

## Open Your Onboarding Issue

- Open an [onboarding issue](https://github.com/gpsaggese/gpsaggese.github.io/issues/new?template=research_onboarding.md)
- Check off each item as you complete it

## Read the Policies

- Read the [policies](/policies)
- Follow [How to Create a PR With Claude Code](pr_process.how_to_guide.md) for
  every task
- Make sure you can dedicate time to the projects

## Read the Must-Read Docs

- Read all the docs in the
  [must-read checklist](/helpers_root/docs/onboarding/all.dev_must_read_checklist.reference.md)
  before you start working
- Read the "Team leaders" section only if you lead a project

## Take the Pledge

- Sign the pledge in the "Pledge" section of your onboarding issue: write the
  number of hours and check each box
- By signing it, you commit to:
  - Send a
    [TODO email](/helpers_root/docs/work_organization/all.todo_email.how_to_guide.md)
    every day
  - Dedicate the hours per week that you promised
  - Not disappear and leave your task in shambles
- If you need to slow down or stop, that is OK, but:
  - Tell us as soon as you know
  - Push your code, even if it is not complete
  - Write in the GitHub issue what is done, what is missing, and what is broken
  - Hand over the task to the person who takes it
