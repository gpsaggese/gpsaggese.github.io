TL;DR: Consistent daily grinding beats sporadic bursts of effort.

<!-- more -->

Grinding is 99.9% of the sucess

The dream of starting a company, finding your dream job, or moving to your
favorite country is at arm's length. The question is: are you going to put in
the work to achieve it?

What people don't tell you is how hard it is. Recently I was up until 2am
working, a 16-hour day, and it was the weekend.

You can't achieve greatness working 9 to 5. This is probably the only thing I
agree on with Elon Musk.

The alternative is 9-9-6: progress every single day. Cut social media. Cancel
Netflix and YouTube. They waste your time and keep you distracted so that you
buy things you don't need.

You should make progress, only on the days you eat. Which is all gooddamn days.

Einstein barricated himselves in a room for 40 days to get the theory of general
relativity done. Food was passed under his door. After that, he was burned out for
several months. Was it worth?

I don't want to sound like a guru, but this is the life I have had for 30 years.
I would do it again and again. It was 110% worth it.

Jocko
