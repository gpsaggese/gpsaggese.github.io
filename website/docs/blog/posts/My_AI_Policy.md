TL;DR: I use LLMs **as human team members** and **despise AI slop**

<!-- more -->

- I use LLMs **in the same way I work with humans** on my team

- I use LLMs to fix my English
    - After 25+ years, sometimes I still prefer to write in Italian and getting
      it translated

- I **review every single line** of code / output from an LLM multiple times in
  the same way I review PRs from junior team members that are smart, but _not
  trustworthy_

- I need to be able to **explain everything** in my projects
    - Every line of code, every comment, every design decision
    - If I can't do that, _I'm using AI improperly_

- Results produced with AI should be of **higher quality** than results without
  it
    - AI is a tool to enhance my work, _not replace my critical thinking_

- I watch out for **becoming dependent** on AI
    - If I can't work without it (e.g., write code, think through problems,
      debug issues), _that's a red flag_
    - AI should augment my capabilities, not let me bypass learning or
      problem-solving

- I use AI for brainstorming, but **exercise critical judgment**
    - I always verify AI suggestions for accuracy
    - I watch out against _hallucinations_
    - I challenge outputs and make informed choices
    - I don't let the machine take over my decision-making or give out to
      AI-lazyiness

- I **abhor AI slop** and anything that looks, tastes, feels like that
    - AI;DR: If I feel something is AI generated, _I lose interest_

- My peers will review my work, so **I am ready to justify** my use of AI and
  defend every part of what I've created

- I expect people that work with me to follow the same or stricter rules
