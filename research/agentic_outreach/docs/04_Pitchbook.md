# How to Find People in Charge of Mergers & Acquisitions (M&A) on PitchBook

## Method 1 — Use People Search with M&A Filters
1.  Log in to PitchBook.

2.  Navigate to **Search → People**.

3.  Apply filters such as:
    - **Job Function:** Corporate Development, Mergers & Acquisitions
    - **Titles:**
      - Head of M&A
      - Director of Corporate Development
      - VP Corporate Development
      - M&A Manager
    - **Keywords:**
      - M&A
      - Mergers
      - Acquisitions

4.  Optionally refine by company type, geography, industry, or deal activity.

5.  View results to see all professionals meeting your criteria.

## Method 2 — Search Companies, Then Identify Their M&A Leads
1.  Go to **Search → Companies**.

2.  Filter companies by industry, size, or geography.

3.  Open a company's profile.

4.  Navigate to **Key People**.

5.  Look for titles such as:
    - Chief Corporate Development Officer
    - Head of Mergers & Acquisitions
    - VP Corporate Development
    - Corporate Strategy & Development Lead

## Method 3 — Use Deal Team Information
1.  Go to **Search → Deals**.

2.  Filter for deal type **M&A**.

3.  Open individual deal pages.

4.  Scroll to the **Deal Team** section.

5.  Identify corporate development leads, bankers, advisors, and legal
    representatives involved.

## Boolean Search Suggestions
Use keyword logic to refine results:

("M&A" OR "Merger" OR "Acquisition") AND ("Director" OR "Head" OR "VP" OR
"Manager")
