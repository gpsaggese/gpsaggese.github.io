# Sprints and coordination for Noesis

- Noesis uses the lightweight weekly sprint described in
  [Lightweight weekly sprint](../../../helpers_root/docs/work_organization/all.scrum.explanation.md#lightweight-weekly-sprint)
- This document has the Noesis specifics

## Cadence

- Each sprint is one week, Thursday through Wednesday
  - The team meets every Thursday to check blockers, review the previous sprint,
    and coordinate the new one
  - Wednesday closes the prior sprint
- The meeting time, timezone, URL, and facilitator remain to be agreed
- The [project process](github_projects_process.reference.md#sprint-process) is
  the source of truth for this cadence

## Goals and backlog

- Product goal: see the [execution plan](../docs/plan.md#mvp-execution-plan)
- E.g., sprint goal: "Evaluate a contract automatically using saved request
  evidence"
- Plan against the actual availability
  - A one-week sprint does not imply weekend work

## Definition of Done

- The definition is in
  [Definition of Done](../../../helpers_root/docs/work_organization/all.scrum.explanation.md#definition-of-done)
- The board uses only Todo, In Progress, Blocked, and Done
  - Ordinary PR review remains In Progress
- For the transitions and the closure, see the
  [GitHub guide](use_github.how_to_guide.md)

## Responsibilities

- The responsibilities are described in
  [Responsibilities](../../../helpers_root/docs/work_organization/all.scrum.explanation.md#responsibilities)
- Confirm the named owners with the team, do not import roles from another
  organization
- Formal Scrum has Product Owner, Scrum Master, and Developers accountabilities
  - They are explained in the
    [official Scrum Guide](https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html)
  - They are not new job titles assigned by this document
