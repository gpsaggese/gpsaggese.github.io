# Organize your Noesis work

- The generic guide is
  [all.organize_your_work.how_to_guide.md](../../../helpers_root/docs/work_organization/all.organize_your_work.how_to_guide.md)
  - It covers the golden rules, the roles, the blockers, and finishing work
- This document has the Noesis specifics

## Cadence

- Use the [Noesis board](https://github.com/users/gpsaggese/projects/2) to make
  work visible
- Sprints last one week, Thursday-Wednesday, with a Thursday meeting to check
  blockers
- The [project process](github_projects_process.reference.md) defines the board
  and the meeting agenda

## Roles

- The roles are described in
  [Roles](../../../helpers_root/docs/work_organization/all.organize_your_work.how_to_guide.md#roles)
- The team chooses the meeting facilitator
  - This document does not assign a person

## Starting work

- Follow
  [Starting work](../../../helpers_root/docs/work_organization/all.organize_your_work.how_to_guide.md#starting-work)
- Read the [onboarding guide](../docs/onboarding.md), the
  [MVP plan](../docs/plans/mvp_plan.md), and the acceptance criteria of the issue

## Recording research and progress

- Follow
  [Keep a Progress Log](../../../helpers_root/docs/planning/all.plan_work.how_to_guide.md#keep-a-progress-log)
- Keep the code and the reviewed documentation in `research/Noesis`
- Record the experiments and the findings in `docs/findings`
- A shared document can supplement the repo notes if everyone can access it
- A Noesis shared Drive folder has not been specified
  - Do not use an inherited KaizenFlow folder
- Keep Git checkouts outside cloud-synced folders, consistent with the onboarding
  guide
