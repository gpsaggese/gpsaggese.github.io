# Design: Rater

## Goal

- Compute the Paper quality rating of one paper from three signals, per
  [`.claude/skills/paper.rules.md`](/.claude/skills/paper.rules.md) `# Paper Rating`
  (PRD
  [`R16`](/research/Estimate_academic_paper_quality/docs/01-prd.md#r16-paper-quality-rating))
  - Score page length, best affiliation, and citations from 1 to 5, or `unknown`
  - Rate the paper with the mean of the known scores, rounded half up
  - Give the value, the score, and the evidence of each signal, and the source and
    date of the citation count
- Pure code: no network, no LLM, no file I/O (the rating functions only; the stage
  `rate` around them reads and writes files)
- Where it fits: the action `rate` of `report_papers.py` -> `_compute_rating()`; its
  inputs come from the features file (the page count, read by `fetcher`, and the
  affiliations, read by `extract_features.py`) and from `bib_sources` (citation
  count). The code lives in `report_papers.py`, with no module of its own (decision
  `D22`)
- Overview:
  [`research/Estimate_academic_paper_quality/docs/02-design.architecture.md`](/research/Estimate_academic_paper_quality/docs/02-design.architecture.md)
- Decisions:
  [`research/Estimate_academic_paper_quality/docs/03-decisions.md`](/research/Estimate_academic_paper_quality/docs/03-decisions.md)
  - Affiliation tiers from a checked-in dated list; the LLM only reads the names
    ([`D15`](/research/Estimate_academic_paper_quality/docs/03-decisions.md#d15-affiliation-tiers-come-from-a-checked-in-dated-list))
  - Citations from Semantic Scholar only, else `unknown`
    ([`D16`](/research/Estimate_academic_paper_quality/docs/03-decisions.md#d16-citations-come-from-semantic-scholar-only))
  - Round half up with `math.floor(mean + 0.5)`
    ([`D17`](/research/Estimate_academic_paper_quality/docs/03-decisions.md#d17-round-half-up-with-mathfloormean--05))

## One Request End to End

- `_compute_rating(inputs, tiers)`:
  - `_score_page_length(n_pages)`, with the table of the rules:

    | Score | Pages        |
    | :---- | :----------- |
    | 5     | 35 or more   |
    | 4     | 20 to 34     |
    | 3     | 10 to 19     |
    | 2     | 5 to 9       |
    | 1     | fewer than 5 |

  - `_score_affiliation(affiliations, tiers)`, for each author, then the best:
    - Normalize the institution: ASCII, lowercase, no punctuation, no leading `the`
    - Score 5 or 4 if the name or an alias of a tier entry is in the institution; the
      longest entry wins, so `University of California, Berkeley` does not match a
      shorter entry
    - Else score by the kind that the LLM read: `central_bank` 5, `university` or
      `research_institute` 3, `company` 2, `none` 1
    - No affiliation in the paper: 1, per the rules
    - The evidence is the quote of the best affiliation
  - `_score_citations(citations, year, today)`:
    - `None`: `unknown`, with a note
    - Age is `today.year - year`, at least `0.5`
    - Age under 2: `count * 2 / age` before scoring, with a note
    - Bands: 5 for 1000 or more, 4 for 300 to 999, 3 for 50 to 299, 2 for 10 to 49, 1
      for fewer than 10
  - Rating: the mean of the known scores, `math.floor(mean + 0.5)`; no known score
    gives `unknown`
- Example: 12 pages (3), a top 20 university (5), 40 citations after 3 years (2):
  mean 3.33, rating 3
- Shape of the tier list (the entries are illustrative):
  ```yaml
  # Filled by hand from the QS site, never from the memory of an LLM.
  as_of: "2026-10-28"
  source: "QS World University Rankings 2026"
  tier_5:
    - name: "Massachusetts Institute of Technology"
      aliases: ["MIT"]
    - name: "National Bureau of Economic Research"
      aliases: ["NBER"]
    - name: "Google DeepMind"
  tier_4:
    - name: "AQR Capital Management"
  ```

## Configuration, Interfaces, and Known Limits

- Configuration: the score tables are constants copied from
  [`.claude/skills/paper.rules.md`](/.claude/skills/paper.rules.md); a test checks
  them against the rules file
- Interfaces:
  ```python
  @dataclasses.dataclass(frozen=True)
  class _RatingInputs:
      # None when the PDF cannot be read.
      n_pages: Optional[int]
      affiliations: Tuple[epqtypes.Affiliation, ...]
      citations: Optional[epqtypes.CitationCount]
      year: int
      today: datetime.date

  def _parse_tiers(data: Dict[str, Any]) -> epqtypes.AffiliationTiers: ...

  def _compute_rating(
      inputs: _RatingInputs, tiers: epqtypes.AffiliationTiers
  ) -> epqtypes.QualityRating: ...
  ```
- Known limits:
  - The tier list ages: a new QS edition needs a hand update and a new `as_of`, then
    `report_papers.py --action rate` rates the papers again with no new LLM call
  - A name that the paper writes in an unusual way (e.g., a department only) misses
    the list and scores 3
  - The kind `central_bank` is a reading of the LLM: the quote lets the user check it

## Deliberately Not Built

- A total score or a ranking of the papers
  ([PRD Non-Goals](/research/Estimate_academic_paper_quality/docs/01-prd.md#non-goals))
- A refresh of the tier list from the QS site: it is a yearly hand task
